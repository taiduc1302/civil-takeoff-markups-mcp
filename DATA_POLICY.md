# Data policy

Nothing in this repository comes from a real project.

- `examples/synthetic_sheet.pdf` is generated by `tools/build_synthetic_sheet.py`. Every street name, lot number, station, elevation, structure and length in it is invented.
- `examples/synthetic_sheet_marked.pdf` is that same sheet after an agent placed 21 native markups on it through an MCP connector. The quantities on those markups were computed by the markup tool.
- `examples/derived_markups.json` is geometry derived from the synthetic sheet.

Do not add to this repository: tender or construction drawings of any kind, even redacted; a tool library or markup palette belonging to a company; rates, quotes, bid values or productivities; registers built from a real project; screenshots that contain any of the above.

Many drawings carry a line saying they may not be reproduced without the owner's consent. Working on such a drawing to prepare the bid it was issued for is what it is for. Publishing it as an example is not, and blurring the street names does not change that.
